01 — About

A bit about me

design eye + a coder's brain

I'm Renzie, a Creative Computing graduate from Bath Spa University (RAK campus), living in Dubai. My degree covered software and web development, AI and data analysis, game development and UX design — and I received honours in two consecutive semesters.

I built a handwritten digit recognizer that runs a Python-trained neural network in the browser, a car dealership website, a tool that helps students track their assignments, a survival game in Unity and an awareness poster on global hunger. Each taught me something different: how to structure a page so it's easy to use, how to plan before writing code, and how to make a message land visually.

Alongside my studies I spent over two years at major Dubai events like GITEX, Big 5 and Gulfood, and coordinating professional training programmes. That taught me to stay calm when live tech fails, handle data carefully and communicate clearly. Now I'd like to bring all of that to a development team.
